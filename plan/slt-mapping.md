The **proposed target** column is written and in place in `course/`, ready to paste into column K against the planner row given. Each one is drawn from what that lesson teaches and what the module's assignment already asks the operator to produce. See `plan/slt-audit.md` for why the current ones do not hold.

Modules 000 and 100 have no lesson bodies in `course/` and are noted at the end.
